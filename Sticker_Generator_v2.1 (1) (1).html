Artwork requirements

Preferred Working PDF standard
  • One sticker / artwork kind per PDF, supplied at 100% scale.
  • Rectangular labels: finished size defined by the PDF TrimBox.
  • Custom shapes: closed vector dieline supplied as a spot colour, preferably named CutContour or Dieline.
  • Bleed should already be present in the Working artwork; a valid BleedBox is preferred.
  • Printable Pantone / spot colours are allowed and must remain printable.
  • Customer crop marks and irrelevant page furniture should be removed or kept outside the production page boxes.
The generator validates and imposes production-ready artwork. It will flag ambiguous files rather than silently guessing how they should be cut.
